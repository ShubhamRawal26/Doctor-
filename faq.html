Appointments, Wait Times & Intake

We guarantee you will be seated in an exam room with your attending physician or care team in under 8 minutes. In the rare event of a medical emergency causing a delay past 15 minutes, your consultation fee is discounted by 50% automatically.

Insurance, Pricing & Payments

We partner directly with over 42 leading domestic and European international health insurance carriers, including Allianz Worldwide Care, Cigna Global, Bupa Global, PZU Zdrowie, AXA Assistance, and Aetna International. For self-pay patients, we provide 100% itemized upfront invoices with no hidden facility surcharges.

Diagnostics, 3T MRI & Laboratory

Our automated Roche Cobas clinical chemistry analyzer delivers standard blood panel and metabolic results in just 45 to 60 minutes. 3T MRI scans are interpreted by dual board-certified radiologists with high-definition DICOM access delivered to your phone portal within 4 hours.
